About Aishwarya

Reality Shows 10+ Years Exp. Topper Multi-Lingual Vocalist

Aishwarya Unni is the Founder of Raag N Rhythm School, a passionate entrepreneur, and a highly dedicated classical singer and music educator with over 10 years of experience. On a mission to make high-quality music education accessible across India, she envisions a platform that empowers students to discover their true musical potential. Known for her soft, melodious, and flawless voice, she brings a deep understanding of Carnatic, Hindustani, Western, and Semi-Classical vocals.

Beyond her academic excellence as a Topper in M.A. Vocal (Classical Music), she has proven her versatility by participating in various reality shows, reaching the finals of the nationally televised Voice of Punjab, and lending her voice to numerous Malayalam albums and commercial jingles. As a top-rated tutor on platforms like UrbanPro, Aishwarya is passionate about nurturing musical talent in students of all ages, approaching every class with immense patience, thorough knowledge, and an encouraging mindset that inspires confidence and a true appreciation for the art of music.
