What's Inside

Powerful. Invisible.

🎵
Scale Selection

All 12 chromatic keys. Tap once to shift the entire resonance. Like retuning a real instrument in your hands.

🎼
Octave Control

Low, Medium, High modes. Match the register of your raga, your voice, your mood. Three worlds, one tap.

🪷
1st String Selection

Pa Sa Sa Sa or Ma Sa Sa Sa — the traditional choice that shapes the entire character and colour of the drone.

⚡
Speed & Volume

Smooth neumorphic sliders. Set the pluck cycle tempo and presence level to match your practice pace.

👆
Swipe Themes

A horizontal swipe transforms the entire visual world — gradient, instrument skin, accent colours — seamlessly.

🔇
Minimal UI

Every control appears when needed, fades when not. The instrument is always the centrepiece. Always.
